description: |
  ReWavier is a listening notebook for anyone who works with audio: music, lessons, meetings, courses, training, interviews, and voice notes.

  Open an audio file, pause, and write a note on the exact moment — minutes, seconds, milliseconds — on the sound wave. Tap +, the track stops, and a bubble shows the time. The marker stays there.

  For creators, bands, teachers, and work. Files stay on the phone. Sign in with Google to use your account and connect Drive too.

  No ads. No social. Just listening and notes on time.
